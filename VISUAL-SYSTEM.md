# USSCALLISTER Visual System

## Palette
- Background: warm cream `#f7f1e8` (never pure white)
- Text: near-black ink `#1a1714`, warm gray secondary `#8a7f72`
- Accent: muted gold `#c4a574` — labels, hover states, email section only

## Typography
- Headlines: Cormorant Garamond, weight 500, tracking tight
- Body and UI: Inter, weight 300 to 500
- No gradients on text, no neon, no playful shapes

## Photography
- Real bodies, natural light, no filters, no stock poses
- Photography is the only texture

## Motion
- Slow and confident: fade-ups on scroll, gentle hover lifts
- One hero zoom that settles over twenty seconds
- Buttons: small, uppercase, letter-spaced, never rounded
- Cards: soft shadows that deepen on hover

## Mood
High-end magazine spread, not a Shopify template. Flat, deliberate, quiet.
